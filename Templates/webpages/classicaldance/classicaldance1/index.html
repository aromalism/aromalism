The gentle, swaying 'Dance of the Enchantress' from Kerala. Characterized by soft torso movements, circular steps, and ivory-gold *Kasavu* attire.

AHARYA ABHINAYA (COSTUME & EMBELLISHMENT)

The Sacred Elegance of Classical Costumes

In Indian classical dance, costume is not merely clothing—it is a sacred bridge connecting the artist to the deity. Every element holds aesthetic and spiritual significance:

Kasavu & Kanchipuram Silks

Pleated Kanchipuram silks for Bharatanatyam and off-white Kasavu gold sarees from Kerala for Mohiniyattam allow fluid *Aramandi* postures.

Temple Jewelry (*Kemp* Sets)

Handcrafted red & green Kemp stones including head ornaments (*Sun & Moon / Nethichutti*), belt (*Odiyanam*), and ear studs (*Jhumka*).

Chilanka (Anklet Bells)

Brass bells woven on leather straps (*Chilanka / Ghungroo*), sanctified during puja and worn to highlight complex *Thalam* (rhythm).

Costume & Makeup Workshops

We train all senior students in authentic stage makeup application, traditional hair ornamentation (*Jathai/Mulla Poo*), and *Alta/Thailam* hand painting.

"Antho Rasah, Hastho Bhavah, Nayano Drishti" — Where the hand goes, the eye follows; where the eye goes, the mind stays.
